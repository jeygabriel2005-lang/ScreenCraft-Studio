## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital editorial and investment authority for Tuxpan, Veracruz, spotlighting premium tourism, regional commerce, maritime infrastructure, and prime real estate. The visual philosophy fuses classic high-fashion editorial craftsmanship with modern business-grade clarity.

### Brand Attributes & Emotional Response
- **Atmospheric Elegance:** Evokes prestige, maritime sophistication, and economic prosperity without ostentation.
- **Editorial Authority:** Delivers deep legibility, architectural layout pacing, and cultural respect for the Veracruz Gulf Coast.
- **Investment Confidence:** Instills trust and clarity for real estate transactions, commercial development briefs, and luxury lifestyle guides.

### Design Movement
**Refined Editorial Minimalism with Architectural Tactility:** Rich, light-toned editorial canvases layered with deep contrast typography, hairline golden dividers, generous whitespace, and restrained micro-interactions that mirror tangible luxury publication print pieces.

## Colors

The palette balances deep contrast, warm editorial print textures, and rich metallic accents while guaranteeing strict compliance with WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility ratios across all interface states.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0D0D0D` - Deep Black):** Serves as high-contrast display text, authoritative body copy on light grounds, and prominent inverted layout panels. Delivers a contrast ratio of >16:1 against the cream canvas.
